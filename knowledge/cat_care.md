# Cat care (general guidance, not veterinary advice)

Task line format: Title | minutes | priority | frequency | essential | typical start | category

## Adult cats
Healthy adult cats (about 2 to 9 years old) need daily meals, a clean litter box, and play to stay active. A cat is usually fed in the morning and played with in the evening.
- Feeding | 10 | high | daily | yes | 08:00 | feeding
- Litter box cleaning | 10 | high | daily | yes | 08:30 | care
- Interactive play | 15 | medium | daily | no | 18:30 | exercise
- Brushing | 5 | low | weekly | no | 19:30 | grooming

## Senior cats
Senior cats (about 10 years and older) usually play more gently and drink less, so watching hydration and comfort matters. Keep the litter box easy to reach for an older cat.
- Feeding | 10 | high | daily | yes | 08:00 | feeding
- Litter box cleaning | 10 | high | daily | yes | 08:30 | care
- Gentle play | 10 | medium | daily | no | 18:30 | exercise
- Hydration check | 5 | medium | daily | no | 19:00 | health

## Kittens
Kittens (under about 1 year old) need several small meals and lots of short play sessions. A young cat is very active and needs a clean litter box every day.
- Kitten feeding | 10 | high | daily | yes | 08:00 | feeding
- Kitten play session | 20 | high | daily | yes | 17:30 | exercise
- Litter box cleaning | 10 | high | daily | yes | 09:00 | care
