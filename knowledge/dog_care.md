# Dog care (general guidance, not veterinary advice)

Task line format: Title | minutes | priority | frequency | essential | typical start | category

## Adult dogs
Healthy adult dogs (about 2 to 7 years old) generally need daily exercise, regular meals and weekly brushing. A common routine for a dog is a longer morning walk, a shorter evening walk and a morning meal.
- Morning walk | 30 | high | daily | yes | 07:30 | exercise
- Evening walk | 20 | medium | daily | no | 18:00 | exercise
- Feeding | 10 | high | daily | yes | 08:00 | feeding
- Brushing | 10 | low | weekly | no | 19:00 | grooming

## Senior dogs
Senior dogs (about 8 years and older) usually do better with shorter, gentler walks spread across the day, and with extra attention to joints and weight. Older dogs often tire quickly, so two short walks beat one long one.
- Gentle morning walk | 15 | high | daily | yes | 07:30 | exercise
- Gentle evening walk | 15 | medium | daily | no | 18:00 | exercise
- Feeding | 10 | high | daily | yes | 08:00 | feeding
- Joint comfort check | 5 | medium | daily | no | 19:30 | health

## Puppies
Puppies (under about 1 year old) need frequent short sessions for play, training and toilet breaks rather than long walks. Young dogs are fed several small meals a day.
- Play and training session | 15 | high | daily | yes | 08:00 | exercise
- Puppy feeding | 10 | high | daily | yes | 08:30 | feeding
- Short toilet break walk | 10 | high | daily | yes | 12:00 | exercise
- Evening play | 15 | medium | daily | no | 18:30 | exercise
