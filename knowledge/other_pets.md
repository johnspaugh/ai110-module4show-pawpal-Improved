## Small and other pets
Small pets such as rabbits, hamsters, guinea pigs and birds need a daily check, food, and fresh water, plus a weekly clean of the cage or habitat. Species differ a lot, so check with a vet or breeder for the details of your animal.

- Habitat cleaning | 15 | medium | weekly | no | 17:00 | care
