# Medication basics (general guidance, not veterinary advice)

## Medication
Medicine, pills and other prescribed treatments (for example arthritis medicine, heartworm pills or flea medication) must follow the vet's instructions exactly. The dose and the times below are placeholders only: PawPal+ never sets a dose, and the owner should replace the start time with the one the vet gave. Giving medication on time every day is essential and should not be skipped to save time.
