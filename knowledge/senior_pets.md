# Senior pets, any species (general guidance, not veterinary advice)

Task line format: Title | minutes | priority | frequency | essential | typical start | category

## Senior pets
Older pets of any species (a senior dog, senior cat or other older animal) benefit from a short daily comfort check: how they move, whether they eat and drink normally, and whether their bed is easy to reach. Changes in mobility or appetite are worth mentioning to a vet.
- Comfort and mobility check | 5 | medium | daily | no | 20:00 | health
- Fresh water and bed check | 5 | medium | daily | no | 20:30 | care
